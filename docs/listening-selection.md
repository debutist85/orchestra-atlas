# Map navigation and listening mix

## State ownership

- `src/store/navigation-store.ts` owns global spatial navigation. Its actions validate family/instrument IDs against the existing map catalog. Entering an instrument derives its family; Back changes only navigation. The URL is a projection of that state (`/strings/cello`); opening a path lands at that zoom and that mix.
- There is no independent listening-selection store. The audible mix is a projection of navigation: orchestra is the full mix, a family or instrument adds those leaf stems as a highlight layer.
- `src/store/catalog.ts` reuses the default seating preset's instrument relationships, including Other → Celesta/Harp. `highlightedInstrumentIds` maps a navigation state to the instruments the engine resolves into focus stem IDs.
- Scene camera interpolation and transient hover remain renderer state. Spatial lighting still follows navigation. Each mesh's `userData.nodeStates` exposes focus and the zoom-derived highlight set per instance. This metadata is a projection, not application state.
- Playback transport lives in `src/store/playback-store.ts`. Load progress lives in `src/store/listening-load-store.ts`. The Web Audio graph stays inside `src/features/listening/listening-engine.ts`.

## Interaction

Map click/tap, spatial labels, Back, and Escape navigate only. Zooming in *is* the mix change.

The top-bar transport plays, pauses, and seeks the shared recording. Play is disabled until the full mix (and catalog metadata) are ready. A draft loading indicator is shown in the player and on the stage during that initial load. Stem chunks are fetched later, when a family or instrument is selected.

Explore remains an independent no-op annotation on the focused instrument group.

## Audio contract

`connectListeningEngine` supplies the current mix and navigation updates. Orchestra / All is continuous `full-orchestra.opus`. A family or instrument highlight keeps that mix playing and adds the leaf stems already listed for the selection as an additive focus layer. Instruments without files (trombone, tuba, harp, celesta, extra percussion) contribute no focus stems; the full mix stays at unity. Rights are not inferred.

Background attenuation lives on `listeningMix`: orchestra 1.0, family 0.25, instrument 0.20. The orchestra is not ducked until the requested focus chunks are ready. Focus gain reuses `relativeInstrumentBoostDb` against activity.json (emphasis 3, cap 24 dB): quiet selected material gets more additional stem level, already-loud or silent material does not. Family intensity is the average of sounding selected instruments, not a stem count. Returning to Orchestra is a gain transition; the media element is not restarted.

The approved contract is [specs/listening.md](../specs/listening.md).

One `AudioContext` is shared. The full mix is an `HTMLMediaElement` routed through that graph. Focus stems use the chunk scheduler (`src/features/listening/chunk-scheduler.ts`). Duration comes from the chunk manifest (or activity profile), not from media element duration alone.

Instrument activity is a precomputed envelope in `public/activity/{excerpt}.json`, generated by `npm run audio:activity -- <excerpt-id>` from the WAV masters. Runtime lookup is `currentTime / sampleInterval` — it does not read PCM or use AnalyserNode. Playback and activity stay separate: the profile describes the musical timeline; transport playing/paused decides whether that lookup is audible.

Master WAV stems are encoded to whole-file Opus with `npm run audio:encode` and to synchronized 15-second chunks with `npm run audio:chunks`. See `docs/audio-assets.md`. The isolated chunk POC remains at `/?chunk-poc` and now uses the same scheduler.

## Validation

`npm test` checks that the mix follows zoom, gain conversion, stem URLs, invalid IDs, subscription cleanup, that playback does not write navigation, and that highlight plans, background gains, and intensity-aware focus gain follow the activity profile. Browser review is required for loading, play/pause/seek, and highlight overlays. The current excerpt files are a local file map only; rights are not recorded here.
